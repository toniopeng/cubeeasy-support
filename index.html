CubeEasy

Support · Last updated: October 29, 2026

CubeEasy is a beginner 3×3 cube tutorial for iPhone. Seven short steps, one screen, and a one-time lifetime unlock after two free course runs. No subscription and no ads.

Contact

Email taopeng46@gmail.com for app problems, purchase help, Restore Purchases questions, or anything else about CubeEasy. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Purchases & Restore

  • The first two full course runs are free. After that, a one-time CubeEasy Lifetime unlock keeps the course.
  • Payment is processed by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your card details.
  • There is no subscription. Lifetime is a single non-consumable purchase.
  • To restore on a new device, open CubeEasy, open the unlock sheet, and tap Restore while signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How CubeEasy works

  • Follow seven lessons on one screen. Tap a move on the face toward you, then Play for one formula cycle, then OK to continue.
  • No account and no sign-in are required.
  • Works with any standard 3×3 puzzle cube. CubeEasy is not affiliated with Rubik's Brand Ltd.

Privacy

In short: CubeEasy does not collect personal data.

  • No accounts, no analytics SDKs, no ads, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • Course progress and unlock status stay on your device (StoreKit / App Store receipt checks with Apple).
  • We do not sell or share your data with data brokers or advertisers.
  • Deleting the app removes local progress on that device. Purchases remain tied to your Apple ID and can be restored later.

Questions about privacy: taopeng46@gmail.com

Troubleshooting

  • If Play or the cube seems stuck, force-quit CubeEasy and reopen it.
  • If Lifetime does not unlock after purchase, tap Restore on the unlock sheet with the same Apple ID.
  • Need a network connection only when buying or restoring through the App Store.
